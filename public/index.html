A LOCAL CONNECTION

Bring your GR III
within reach.

The bridge runs on this computer and reads JPEGs from the camera over Wi-Fi.

  1. 1

    Turn on the camera’s Wi-Fi

    Enable wireless LAN on your Ricoh GR III.

  2. 2

    Join it on the bridge computer

    Use your operating system’s Wi-Fi settings to connect to the camera network. Internet access may pause.

  3. 3

    Try the local connection

    The bridge will contact 192.168.0.1. This real-camera path has not been tested with hardware.

No Bluetooth pairing is performed here. Bluetooth is not used to transfer originals.
